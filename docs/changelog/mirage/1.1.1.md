### New

- The keypose, Constants, curve, and modulation editors now have a pin button beside Close (or press K). Pinned, the default, an editor stays open until you close it. Unpinned, a click anywhere outside it closes it. The choice is remembered
- Choose any installed shader template as the default for new Mirage effects and rack entries
- Filter the shader browser by Audio to find every shader that reacts to your clip's sound, including community shaders you haven't downloaded yet

### Improved

- The editors, template browser, and grading panel now have a denser backing across the whole panel, header included, so their controls stay readable over bright footage
- The current default template is easy to spot in the template browser: its card has a subtle animated border and a filled pin
- Hover any card in the template browser and click its pin to make that template the default
- Made the vectorscope track colour-surface puck drags smoothly, including fast, large adjustments
- The code editor now shows a missing `// #template` line as an amber warning with Save disabled instead of a red error, since the shader still renders without it
- Kai no longer treats a position control's value as a 0-1 coordinate when writing shaders - the reference it works from now spells out that positions arrive in pixels, a mixup that could produce a valid shader whose output was just black
- Kai's shader reference is now explicit about what every control kind delivers: percentage fields inside a multi-value control arrive already divided by 100, and colour swatches arrive in the same encoding as the footage, so its shaders get the math right the first time
- Kai now compiles every shader it writes and repairs compile errors itself before the code reaches the editor, follows stricter GLSL rules so its shaders avoid reserved words, per-frame state and effects too subtle to see, and no longer reports success after doing nothing when a request to make a new filter or effect was mistaken for an animation
- An open keypose editor now lets you briefly scrub elsewhere before returning to its keypose, without snapping the playhead back during playback
- The Advanced timeline now previews what a modifier click will do: hold Cmd over a gap for the copy cursor to insert a keypose there, or Option over a keypose for the not-allowed cursor to delete it
- Layer and category headers on the Advanced timeline now collapse only from a click on their name or chevron, so a click on the header's empty space no longer folds the group by accident

### Fixed

- Fixed the scrollbar in the code editor's error strip covering the message while scrolling
- Fixed the keypose, Constants, curve, and modulation editors reopening away from where you left them
- Fixed the keypose and Constants editors reopening at their natural height instead of the height you had resized them to
- Fixed colour pickers disappearing from rack-hosted grading shaders such as Qualifier
- Fixed qualifier selection mattes in the main viewer showing every key instead of the selected puck's key
- Fixed the Original, Matte and Split pill bar disappearing from the main viewer when shader controls were visible
- Fixed Original and Split keyboard shortcuts being disabled in compact mode when a generator was selected later in a source-processing rack
- Fixed the main viewer rendering Plasma after deleting the first of two rack shaders
- Fixed on-screen-control cursors being replaced by the arrow in the mini viewer
- Fixed audio visualizers and other generator shaders with transparency turning the clip behind them black - they now draw over your footage and stay truly transparent where the footage itself is transparent
- Fixed the L key popping up the accent menu instead of typing while an editor panel was open, including in the Kai prompt
- Fixed the main viewer staying in Matte after the toggle was switched off when a grading shader was the only entry in the rack, and while motion blur was enabled
- Fixed the mini viewer never showing the matte for a rack-hosted grading shader even with Matte enabled
- Fixed the vectorscope shifting its measurements on pressing or releasing a puck without moving it - the scope now measures at one resolution and sample density through presses, drags and release
- Fixed the mini viewer preview softening while dragging a colour-surface puck
- Fixed a lane with no keyposes drawing as an empty row on the Advanced timeline instead of showing its dashed grey placeholder line
- Fixed the ring highlighting the keypose an open editor is editing staying visible over the ruler and labels after that keypose was scrolled or zoomed out of view
