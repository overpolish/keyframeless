---
id: core
summary: A layer's shape and points
---

- The Core group is the shape itself, the outline you draw.
  - **Points** - the points along the outline, and the handles that curve them. Edit them with the pen tool: drag a point to move it, drag a handle to bend the line, or double-click a point to switch it between a sharp corner and a smooth curve. Animate Points and the shape morphs smoothly from one outline into another.

Make a shape by drawing with the pen tool, dragging out a rectangle or ellipse, importing an SVG, or tracing an image. Images and groups have no points to edit, so they show the move, scale and rotate handles instead.
