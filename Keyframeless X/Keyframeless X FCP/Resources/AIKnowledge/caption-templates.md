---
id: caption-templates
summary: Caption templates from the Keyframeless FxPlug plugins - Title-mode only
---

Caption templates are styled, animated title designs published by the Keyframeless community and developers. Templates only apply when the caption type is **Title** - Caption-format exports use FCP's built-in caption styling instead.

**Where templates come from.**

- **Built-in** - shipped with each installed Keyframeless plugin. Built-in FCP basic title.
- **Custom** - `.moti` files dragged into the template picker by the user (stored locally).
- **Community** - curated templates from the `overpolish/keyframeless-community` GitHub repository, browsable inside the picker with an "Install" button.

**Picker features.** Search by name or author, filter to favourites only, filter to per-word-animation templates only, star/unstar favourites. Custom templates can be right-clicked to uninstall. Community templates show update notifications when a newer version is available.

**Per-template parameters.** Some templates publish custom parameters (text size, colour palettes, animation timing, etc.) via the "Customize Available Parameters" modal. These persist per-template.

**Per-word animation.** Templates that opt into per-word animation animate each word independently (e.g. one word pops in at a time). Hidden in the picker when SRT cues are imported (SRT has per-cue, not per-word, timing).
