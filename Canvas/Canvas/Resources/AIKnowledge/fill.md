---
id: fill
summary: Fill a shape or tint an image - solid, gradient, hachure
---

- The Fill group colours in the inside of a shape, or tints an image. Turn it on with **Fill Enabled**.
  - **Colour** - a single colour or a gradient, with a **Fill Angle** to point the gradient whichever way you like.
  - **Fill Style** - a flat colour, or a hand-drawn **hachure** (sketchy shading made of hatched lines). Hachure adds **Fill Gap** (how far apart the lines are) and **Fill Weight** (how thick they are).
  - **Fill Amount** - on an image, how strongly the fill tints it (0 leaves it untouched, 100 is a full tint). Shapes are always filled solid, so this only matters for images.

The fill sits under the stroke. On a shape it colours the inside, and on an image it lays a tint or a bit of sketchy shading over the picture.
