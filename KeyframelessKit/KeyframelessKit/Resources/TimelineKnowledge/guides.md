---
id: guides
summary: Interactive walkthroughs for learning workflows
---

Plugins ship with interactive guides that walk you through workflows step by step inside the inspector itself. Guides highlight specific controls with an amber glow and explain what to do at each step. Some steps include magnetic snap-to-target hints to land you on the value the guide is teaching.

Open guides from the Help (?) button in the logo banner at the top of the inspector.

Guides are the fastest way to learn the workflow if you're new to the plugin, and they always reflect the current UI.
