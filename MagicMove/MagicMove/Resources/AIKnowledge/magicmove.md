---
id: magicmove
summary: What Magic Move does
---

Magic Move is a Final Cut Pro plugin that animates a clip's position, scale, rotation, and opacity. Drop it on a clip from the Effects browser.

Because Magic Move's transforms happen inside the clip's frame, it works best on a compound clip or an adjustment clip wrapping the footage you want to move - that way the move can extend past the original clip's edges without being cropped. The plugin reminds you of this when needed.
