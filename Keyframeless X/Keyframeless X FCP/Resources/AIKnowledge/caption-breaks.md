---
id: caption-breaks
summary: Manual caption breaks (blue lines) - right-click between words to force line wrap
---

Caption breaks are manual line-wrap points that override the automatic "Max Words Per Line" wrapping during export. They appear as **blue vertical lines** between words in a sentence row.

**How to set one.** Right-click between two words in a sentence row in the Edit tab. A blue line appears at that position. Right-click the line again to remove it.

**Persistence.** Caption breaks survive across sessions per clip and sentence.

**Export behaviour.** When the user exports captions, each break forces a line wrap at that word position, regardless of the Max Words Per Line setting. Useful for getting readable line breaks at clause boundaries that automatic wrapping would miss.
