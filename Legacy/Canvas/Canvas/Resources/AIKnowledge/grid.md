---
id: grid
summary: The overlay grid and snapping - toggle, spacing, snap
---

- The grid controls live on the small toolbar that floats over the preview.
  - **Grid** - shows or hides a grid over the whole preview to help you line things up. Off to start.
  - **Auto grid spacing** - widens the grid squares as you zoom out, so the grid never gets too dense to see. Turn it off to keep the squares one fixed size. On to start.
  - **Cell size** - sets how big the grid squares are: 10, 20, 50 or 100 pixels. The button shows the current size.
  - **Snap to grid** - makes a layer you drag jump neatly onto the nearest grid line, so things line up. Hold **Cmd** while dragging to ignore it for that one move. Off to start.

The same toolbar appears in both the main viewer and the small preview in the inspector. It is only there to help you while you work and never shows up in your finished video.
