### New

- **Sonar** - a new free tab that makes effects react to your audio: drop in a project, pick the clips it should listen to, and hit Publish
- What you select is what the effect hears
- Effects stay in sync on export, where audio-reactive effects normally come out wrong or frozen, because Sonar listens to the audio up front rather than during the render
- Uses the audio as Final Cut Pro plays it, so volume changes, fades, compressors and EQ are all included
- **Trial and licensing** - Steno can now be tried before buying: unlicensed, the full flow works but exports stop at the first 5 captions or titles
- Activating is one time: click Trial in the top bar, paste the license key from your Payhip receipt, and everything exports - no subscription, no re-activation

### Improved

- Standard Final Cut Pro role colours now signal the audio type on the timeline
- Clicking a clip to hear it now plays straight away instead of pausing for about a second first
- The playhead now follows what you hear rather than running slightly ahead
- The transcription model list now shows each model's real download size and the memory it needs, like Kai's model list - the Parakeet models previously claimed ~600 MB when their downloads are really 2.6-3 GB

### Fixed

- Temporary audio is now cleared out automatically - working files were never removed and could quietly build to many gigabytes over months, along with part-finished transcription model downloads
- Fixed the Parakeet model download looking stuck: it ran a spinner with no numbers for a multi-gigabyte download - it now shows real percentage progress, and picks up where it left off if interrupted
