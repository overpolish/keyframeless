---
id: fill
summary: Fill a shape or tint an image - solid, gradient, hachure
---

The Fill group paints the inside of a shape, or tints / overlays an image. Turn it on with **Fill Enabled**.

- **Colour** - a solid colour or a gradient (with a **Fill Angle**), shared with the kit colour control.
- **Fill Style** - solid, or a hand-drawn **hachure** (cross-hatch / sketchy shading). Hachure exposes **Fill Gap** (line spacing) and **Fill Weight** (line thickness).
- **Fill Amount** - on an image layer, how strongly the fill tints the image (0 = untouched, 100 = full tint). A vector shape is always filled solid, so this applies to images only.

A fill draws under the stroke. On a vector path the fill is the shape's interior; on an image it is a tint or a hachure overlay keyed to the image.
